Al Ahly

introduction

Al Ahly Sporting Club (Arabic: النادي الأهلي للرياضة البدنية), commonly known as Al Ahly (Arabic: الأهلي, lit. 'the national'), is a professional sports club based in Cairo, Egypt. The club is mainly known for its professional football team which plays in the Egyptian Premier League, the highest tier in the Egyptian league system. They are known to be the most successful club in world football based on league titles.


History

Founded on 24 April 1907, the club is renowned for its consistent success at both domestic and continental levels, regularly contending in CAF tournaments. Al Ahly has a record of 45 Egyptian Premier League titles, 39 Egypt Cup titles, and 16 Egyptian Super Cups. In international competitions, Al Ahly has won a record 12 CAF Champions League titles, 1 CAF Confederation Cup, a record of 8 CAF Super Cups, a record of 4 African Cup Winners' Cups, 1 Afro-Asian Club Championship, 1 Arab Club Champions Cup, 1 Arab Cup Winners' Cup, a record of 2 Arab Super Cups, and has won 4 bronze medals in the FIFA Club World Cup.

Best Ahly Players

  1. Mahmoud Al khatib

    2. At 16 years old Al-Khatib played with the under-18 team, and his first match was against his former club, Al-Nasr. He was promoted to the first team and played his first official match against Al-Balaastik Club on October 15, 1972. In the same match, he scored his first goal, and from then on, goals kept coming in the league until he reached 108 league goals by the day of his retirement, scored in 199 matches during 17 consecutive years of playing.



  2. Mohamed Aboutrika

    3. Mohamed Mohamed Aboutrika (born 7 November 1978) is a former Egyptian international football player who played as an attacking midfielder and second striker . He is known for being a playmaker who contributes to the attack and scoring goals, in addition to his magical touches, dribbling and penetration skills, and his playing style has been compared to the famous French player Zinedine Zidane . He began his football career playing for Tersana Club as a youth player, then moved up to the first team, helping them gain promotion to the Egyptian Premier League. He played with them for four seasons before transferring to Al Ahly Club , where he achieved a series of consecutive titles and accomplishments both domestically and internationally. His most notable achievements include a bronze medal at the 2006 FIFA Club World Cup, where he was the tournament's top scorer. He also won five CAF Champions League titles ( 2005 , 2006 , 2008 , 2012 , and 2013 ), four CAF Super Cups, seven league titles, three Egyptian Cups, and four Egyptian Super Cups with Al Ahly. In 2012, he moved to Baniyas on loan, winning the Gulf Club Champions Cup with them. He returned to Al Ahly in 2013 and won his fifth CAF Champions League title with the club. After the tournament, he announced that the FIFA Club World Cup would be his final appearance in the world of football.
